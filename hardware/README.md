# Hardware

ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 + SIMCom A7670E-LASE gateway board, KiCad 10.

- 4 layers, 99.9 x 99.9 mm, JLC04161H-7628 stackup, ENIG
- Open `sms.kicad_pro`. The JLC symbols, footprints and 3D models are in `lib/`.
- `schematic-notes.md` has the design notes that used to sit as text on the schematic sheets.
- `fab/rev0.6/` holds the Gerbers, BOM and CPL used for the JLCPCB order.

PCB fabrication and assembly sponsored by JLCPCB.
